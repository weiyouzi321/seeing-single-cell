Quality Control

Before analysis, low-quality cells must be removed — empty droplets, damaged cells, and doublets. Filter based on nCount, nFeature, and mitochondrial percentage.

Key QC Metrics

Each cell produces three key metrics: nCount_RNA (total UMIs), nFeature_RNA (number of genes detected), percent.mt (mitochondrial gene percentage).

nCount_RNA

Total UMIs

Reflects cellular RNA content; too low = empty droplet, too high = doublet

Recommended: > 1000
nFeature_RNA

Number of genes

Number of genes detected per cell; too low = poor quality

Recommended: 200–2500
percent.mt

Mitochondrial %

Indicator of cell stress/apoptosis; high = damaged cells

Recommended: < 5%
